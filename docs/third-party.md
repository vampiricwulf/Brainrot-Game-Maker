# Third-party code

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

Two libraries are bundled into the app unchanged. **ℹ About** lists them too, with omggif's license.

## mediabunny

- [mediabunny](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny) 1.61.6
- © 2026-present Vanilagy and contributors
- License: Mozilla Public License 2.0. Its source is at github.com/Vanilagy/mediabunny.

It reads videos and sounds, and writes converted, edited and smaller copies, using the browser's own video and sound
encoders (WebCodecs). **🔄 Convert**, **✎ Edit**, **🗜 Make smaller…** and the check whether a video or sound plays
here all use it.
